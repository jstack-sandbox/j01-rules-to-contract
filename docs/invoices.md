# Invoices

### INVOICE-002 — an invoice total is rounded to cents

**Status:** active

an invoice total is rounded to cents.

**Example:** a total of 10.005 is shown as 10.01

### REMIND-001 — a reminder is sent seven days after an invoice is due

**Status:** planned

a reminder is sent seven days after an invoice is due.

**Example:** an invoice due on 3 November gets a reminder on 10 November

### REMIND-002 — a paid invoice gets no reminder

**Status:** planned

a paid invoice gets no reminder.

**Example:** an invoice paid on 5 November gets no reminder
